Keys while this view has focus: arrow keys turn the model, plus and minus zoom, 0 resets the view, Space pauses or plays, A turns auto-turn on or off, L shows or hides labels, N steps through the parts of this plate and reads what each is, Enter acts on the current part, left and right square brackets move to the previous or next plate. Alt plus Shift plus K turns these keys on for the whole page, and Alt plus Shift plus S opens the settings menu. A text explanation of the plate follows the controls.
